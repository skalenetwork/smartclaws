SmartClaws · architecture at a glance

Sensors, agents, and a record neither can rewrite.

An IoT stack where the SKALE blockchain replaces the cloud broker. Devices publish what they measured, controllers publish what they decided, and the permissions live in the contracts. A channel can also be sealed, so only its readers can open it.

01The whole system, one loop

Every message between hardware and controller crosses the chain. Nothing is exchanged off the record, and no component talks to another directly.

02What sits on the chain

Four kinds of object, all created through one registry. Every device and every agent owns exactly two channels — one it writes, one that is written to it — and is registered plain or encrypted, once, for both.

03What a channel is

A log that only grows. Writing is gated by role; reading is open to all. Every message carries the same small envelope — whatever the device, and whatever the channel kind.

04What an encrypted channel hides

Encryption here hides the reading, not the fact of one. The payload is sealed before it leaves the bridge and is only ever stored sealed — but the transaction that carried it is an ordinary public transaction, and everything around the payload stays legible to anyone watching the chain.

05Who holds which key

Authority isn't a setting an agent can talk its way around — it's granted by the owner's wallet, and without it the contract rejects the transaction. Encrypted channels add a second, separate axis: who may write is a role, and who may read is a list.

Reading is the other axis, and it is built out of different parts. A reader is not granted a role; it is added to a list kept on the channel itself. And what it needs in order to open what it is sent is a viewing key — which need not be the key that spends.

06Optional — proving which device spoke

A wallet proves who was allowed to write. It doesn't prove which physical machine produced the reading. Where an agent runs on a host with a Vodafone Pairpoint authenticated SIM, the network vouches for that hardware's identity and it can sign its own data — so the payload can carry a signature alongside the reading.

07Optional — where the agent thinks

The chain records what an agent did; where it reasons is a separate choice, and it's yours — model, provider and harness. With a private inference provider — we recommend NEAR AI Cloud — the model runs inside a hardware enclave, so prompts and responses can't be read by the host or the provider, and aren't retained for training.

08What ships

One shared service layer under three ways to reach it, so what an operator does by hand and what an agent does automatically stay in step.
